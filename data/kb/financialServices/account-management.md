# Account Management

## Account Types
**AusFin Everyday Plus Transaction Account:**
- Monthly fee: $5.00 (waived with $2,000+ monthly deposit, under 25, or eligible pension/benefit recipients)
- Visa Debit card included
- Unlimited free AusFin ATM withdrawals
- Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay compatible
- Real-time transaction notifications via MyAusFin app

**AusFin Everyday Saver Account:**
- No monthly fee
- 4 free withdrawals per month ($2.00 per additional withdrawal)
- Visa Debit card included
- Ideal for customers who prefer a no-fee account with limited transactions

**AusFin Online Savings Account:**
- No monthly fee
- Variable interest rate: 5.10% p.a. on balances up to $250,000
- Bonus interest of 0.50% p.a. when you deposit $200+ per month and make no withdrawals
- No Visa Debit card — transfers to/from linked AusFin transaction account only
- Interest calculated daily, paid monthly

**AusFin Premium Transaction Account:**
- Monthly fee: $10.00 (waived with $4,000+ monthly deposit)
- Unlimited domestic and international ATM fee rebates
- Complimentary travel insurance for overseas purchases
- Priority customer service line

## Opening a New Account
**Online (MyAusFin app or website):**
1. Visit www.ausfin.com.au or download the MyAusFin app
2. Select the account type and click "Open Account"
3. Provide your full name, date of birth, residential address, and contact details
4. Verify your identity using your Australian driver's licence or passport (digital verification)
5. Set your 4-digit security PIN and online banking password
6. Account is typically active within minutes; Visa Debit card posted within 5-7 business days

**In-branch:**
1. Visit any AusFin branch with valid photo ID (driver's licence or passport)
2. Provide your Tax File Number (TFN) — optional but recommended to avoid withholding tax on interest
3. Account opened same day; card issued in-branch or posted within 5-7 business days

**Eligibility:**
- Must be 14+ years old (under 18 requires parental consent for some products)
- Must be an Australian citizen, permanent resident, or hold an eligible visa
- Must provide a valid Australian residential address

## Closing an Account
**How to close:**
1. Ensure all pending transactions, direct debits, and scheduled payments are cancelled or redirected
2. Transfer any remaining balance to another account
3. Call AusFin on 13 22 65 or visit a branch to request closure
4. Account closure confirmed in writing within 5 business days

**Important notes:**
- Accounts with linked loans or credit cards cannot be closed until those products are settled or transferred
- Any accrued interest on savings accounts is paid out at closure
- Visa Debit card is automatically cancelled upon account closure
- AusFin retains account records for 7 years as required by law

## Joint Accounts
**Setting up a joint account:**
- Available on all AusFin transaction and savings accounts
- Both account holders must provide identification and sign the application
- Joint accounts can be set up as "both to sign" or "either to sign" for transactions
- Both holders receive a Visa Debit card (transaction accounts)

**Managing a joint account:**
- Both holders have equal access to view balances and transaction history
- "Either to sign" accounts allow either holder to make withdrawals and payments independently
- "Both to sign" accounts require both holders to authorise transactions over a set threshold
- To remove a joint holder, both parties must provide written consent (or a court order)

## Statements and Transaction History
**Statement options:**
- **eStatements (default):** Available in the MyAusFin app and online banking, retained for 7 years
- **Paper statements:** Available on request, $2.50 per statement posted
- Statement frequency: Monthly for transaction accounts, quarterly for savings accounts
- Transaction history searchable by date, amount, or merchant in MyAusFin

**Requesting historical statements:**
- Up to 7 years of statements available via MyAusFin online banking
- Older statements: Request via branch or call centre ($10 per statement for archived records)

## Direct Debits and Recurring Payments
**Setting up a direct debit:**
1. Provide the biller with your AusFin BSB (062-000) and account number
2. Or set up via MyAusFin app under "Payments > Direct Debits"
3. Authorise the direct debit agreement with the biller

**Managing direct debits:**
- View all active direct debits in MyAusFin app
- Cancel a direct debit by contacting the biller directly or through AusFin
- Set up direct debit alerts to be notified before each deduction
- Dispute an unauthorised direct debit within 7 years of the transaction (contact AusFin to initiate)

## BPAY
**Paying bills with BPAY:**
1. Log in to MyAusFin app or online banking
2. Select "Pay > BPAY"
3. Enter the biller code and your customer reference number (found on your bill)
4. Enter the payment amount and select the account to pay from
5. Confirm the payment — BPAY payments are typically processed same business day

**BPAY limits:**
- Daily BPAY limit: $10,000 (can be increased by calling AusFin)
- No fee for BPAY payments from AusFin accounts

## MyAusFin App Features
**Key features:**
- **Account overview:** View balances, recent transactions, and upcoming payments
- **Instant transfers:** Transfer between AusFin accounts instantly, PayID transfers to other banks
- **Card management:** Lock/unlock cards, set spending limits, order replacement cards
- **Spending insights:** Categorised spending breakdown, monthly budgets, and savings goals
- **Notifications:** Real-time push notifications for transactions, low balance alerts, and payment reminders
- **Biometric login:** Fingerprint and Face ID for secure, quick access
- **Digital wallet:** Add your AusFin Visa Debit or Credit card to Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Samsung Pay
- **Branch and ATM locator:** Find the nearest AusFin branch or ATM

**Supported devices:**
- iOS 15.0 or later (iPhone and iPad)
- Android 10.0 or later
- Download from the App Store or Google Play

## Regulatory Information
- AusFin transaction and savings accounts are protected under the **Financial Claims Scheme (FCS)**, which guarantees deposits up to $250,000 per account holder per ADI
- AusFin is an Authorised Deposit-taking Institution (ADI) regulated by **APRA**
- Account terms and conditions are governed by the **Banking Code of Practice** administered by the **Australian Banking Association (ABA)**
- For complaints, contact AusFin Customer Relations on 1300 308 008 or lodge with **AFCA** on 1800 931 678
