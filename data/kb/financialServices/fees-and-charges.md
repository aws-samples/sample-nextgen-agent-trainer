# Fees and Charges

## Account-Keeping Fees
**AusFin Everyday Plus Transaction Account:**
- Monthly account-keeping fee: $5.00
- Fee waived when $2,000 or more is deposited into the account each calendar month
- Fee waived for customers under 25 years of age
- Fee waived for customers receiving an eligible government pension or benefit

**AusFin Everyday Saver Account:**
- No monthly account-keeping fee
- Limited to 4 free withdrawals per month; $2.00 per additional withdrawal
- No minimum deposit requirement

**AusFin Premium Transaction Account:**
- Monthly account-keeping fee: $10.00
- Includes unlimited domestic and international ATM fee rebates
- Includes complimentary travel insurance when used for overseas purchases
- Fee waived when $4,000 or more is deposited each calendar month

## ATM Fees
**AusFin ATM network:**
- Free unlimited withdrawals at AusFin ATMs Australia-wide
- Free balance inquiries at AusFin ATMs

**Non-AusFin ATMs (domestic):**
- $2.50 per withdrawal at non-AusFin ATMs
- $1.00 per balance inquiry at non-AusFin ATMs
- AusFin Premium Account holders: all domestic ATM fees rebated

**International ATMs:**
- $5.00 per withdrawal at international ATMs
- 3% international transaction fee on the converted AUD amount
- AusFin Premium Account holders: international ATM withdrawal fee waived (3% conversion fee still applies)

## International Transaction Fees
- **Visa Debit/Credit purchases overseas:** 3% of the AUD transaction amount
- **Online purchases in foreign currency:** 3% of the AUD transaction amount
- **AusFin Travel Card:** No international transaction fees (separate product)
- Currency conversion uses the Visa wholesale exchange rate at the time of transaction

## Fee Waivers and Refunds
**Eligible fee waivers:**
- Account-keeping fees waived for eligible concession card holders (Pensioner Concession Card, Health Care Card)
- First-time fee reversal available as a one-time goodwill gesture for long-standing customers
- Fees incurred due to AusFin system errors are automatically reversed

**Requesting a fee reversal:**
1. Call AusFin on 13 22 65 or visit a branch
2. Explain the circumstances of the fee
3. Agent will review account history and eligibility
4. If approved, credit applied within 1-2 business days
5. One goodwill reversal per 12-month period per account

## Fee Schedules by Product
**Credit Cards:**
- **AusFin Low Rate Visa:** $59/year annual fee, 13.99% p.a. purchase rate, 21.99% p.a. cash advance rate
- **AusFin Visa Platinum:** $249/year annual fee, 20.99% p.a. purchase rate, includes travel insurance and lounge access
- **AusFin Rewards Visa:** $149/year annual fee, 19.99% p.a. purchase rate, 1 AusFin Reward point per $1 spent
- Late payment fee: $30 per missed minimum payment
- Over-limit fee: $10 (charged once per statement period)
- Cash advance fee: $3.50 or 2% of the amount (whichever is greater)

**Personal Loans:**
- Establishment fee: $250 (one-off)
- Monthly service fee: $10
- Early repayment fee: Nil for variable rate loans; break cost may apply for fixed rate loans
- Late payment fee: $20 per missed repayment

**Home Loans:**
- Application fee: $600 (may be waived for loans over $500,000)
- Valuation fee: $300 per property
- Monthly service fee: $10 (waived for AusFin Complete Home Loan Package at $395/year)
- Discharge fee: $350 per loan
- Break cost: Applies to fixed rate loans repaid early — calculated based on remaining term and rate differential

## Regulatory Information
- All AusFin fees are disclosed in the relevant Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) and Key Fact Sheet
- Fee changes are communicated with at least 30 days' written notice as required by ASIC
- If you believe a fee has been incorrectly charged, contact AusFin on 13 22 65 or lodge a complaint through the **Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA)** on 1800 931 678
