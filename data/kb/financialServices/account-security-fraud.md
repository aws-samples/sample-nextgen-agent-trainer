# Account Security and Fraud Protection

## Reporting Fraud or Suspicious Transactions
**If you suspect fraud on your AusFin account:**
1. Call the AusFin Fraud Hotline immediately: 1800 888 333 (24/7)
2. Log in to the MyAusFin app and tap "Report Suspicious Activity" under the Security menu
3. Request an immediate card block if a credit or debit card is compromised
4. Note the date, amount, and merchant name of any suspicious transactions
5. File a police report if instructed by the fraud team

**AusFin will never:**
- Ask for your full PIN, password, or OTP via phone, email, or SMS
- Send links asking you to verify your account details
- Request remote access to your computer or phone

## Card Blocking and Replacement
**Blocking a compromised card:**
- Call 1800 888 333 or use the MyAusFin app to instantly block your card
- Temporary card lock is available for misplaced cards (can be unlocked if found)
- Permanent block required for confirmed fraud — card is cancelled and cannot be reactivated

**Replacement card process:**
- Standard replacement: 5-7 business days by registered post
- Express replacement: 1-2 business days ($15 express fee, waived for confirmed fraud)
- Temporary digital card issued immediately via MyAusFin app for online purchases
- New card number issued — update any recurring direct debits and subscriptions

## Suspicious Transaction Investigation
**Investigation process:**
1. AusFin logs the disputed transaction and assigns a case number
2. Provisional credit may be applied to your account within 48 hours for amounts over $100
3. Investigation conducted under the ePayments Code — typically resolved within 21 business days
4. For complex cases involving overseas merchants, resolution may take up to 45 business days
5. You will be notified of the outcome by phone and in writing

**Customer liability:**
- Under the ePayments Code, you are generally not liable for unauthorised transactions if you have not contributed to the loss
- Liability may apply if your PIN was shared or written down, or if you unreasonably delayed reporting
- AusFin covers losses from card skimming, data breaches, and system errors at no cost to you

## Identity Verification for Account Changes
**Required for sensitive account changes:**
- Full name and date of birth
- Account number or card number
- 4-digit security PIN
- One-time password (OTP) sent to registered mobile for high-risk changes

**High-risk changes requiring enhanced verification:**
- Updating registered mobile number or email address
- Adding or removing account signatories
- Increasing credit card limits
- Changing direct debit authorities
- Requesting account closure

## Scam Awareness and Prevention
**Common scams targeting AusFin customers:**
- **Phishing emails/SMS:** Messages impersonating AusFin asking you to click a link and enter login details
- **Phone scams:** Callers claiming to be from AusFin requesting your PIN, password, or OTP
- **Investment scams:** Unsolicited offers promising high returns with low risk, often using AusFin branding
- **Remote access scams:** Callers asking you to install software to "fix" a problem with your account

**How to protect yourself:**
- Never share your PIN, password, or OTP with anyone — AusFin staff will never ask for these
- Enable transaction notifications in the MyAusFin app
- Use strong, unique passwords for online banking
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on your MyAusFin account
- Regularly review your statements for unfamiliar transactions
- Report suspicious communications to AusFin and to Scamwatch (www.scamwatch.gov.au)

## AusFin Security Features
**Built-in protections:**
- **Real-time fraud monitoring:** AI-powered transaction monitoring flags unusual activity 24/7
- **SMS/push alerts:** Instant notifications for transactions over $100 or flagged as suspicious
- **Card lock:** Instantly lock and unlock your card via the MyAusFin app
- **Secure online banking:** 256-bit encryption, session timeouts, and device recognition
- **Biometric login:** Fingerprint and Face ID supported on the MyAusFin app

## Regulatory Information
- AusFin is regulated by the **Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA)** and holds an Australian Financial Services Licence (AFSL) issued by **ASIC**
- Fraud disputes are handled under the **ePayments Code** administered by ASIC
- If you are not satisfied with the outcome of a fraud investigation, you can lodge a complaint with the **Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA)** on 1800 931 678 or at www.afca.org.au
