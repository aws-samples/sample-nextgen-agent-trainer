## Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI)
**When LMI applies:**
- Required when borrowing more than 80% of the property value (LVR above 80%)
- One-off premium paid at settlement or capitalised into the loan
- Protects the lender (not the borrower) in case of default

**Estimated LMI costs (indicative only):**
- LVR 81-85%: Approximately $2,500-$8,000 depending on loan amount
- LVR 86-90%: Approximately $8,000-$18,000 depending on loan amount
- LVR 91-95%: Approximately $18,000-$35,000 depending on loan amount

**How to avoid or reduce LMI:**
- Save a 20% deposit to keep LVR at or below 80%
- Use a family guarantee (AusFin Family Pledge) where a family member's property secures the shortfall
- Check eligibility for the First Home Guarantee scheme (limited places per financial year)

## Offset Accounts
**How offset accounts work:**
- A transaction account linked to your home loan
- The balance in the offset account reduces the loan principal used to calculate interest
- Example: $500,000 loan with $50,000 in offset = interest calculated on $450,000
- Available on AusFin variable rate home loans and the variable portion of split loans
- No limit on the offset account balance
- Full transaction account functionality — use for everyday banking

## Redraw Facility
**How redraw works:**
- Access extra repayments you have made above the minimum required amount
- Available on AusFin variable rate home loans
- Minimum redraw amount: $500
- No fee for redraw via MyAusFin app or online banking
- $50 fee for redraw processed in-branch
- Redraw is not available on fixed rate loans during the fixed period

## First Home Owner Grant (FHOG)
**Eligibility (varies by state/territory):**
- Must be purchasing or building a new home (not established/existing in most states)
- Property value caps apply (e.g., $750,000 in NSW, $750,000 in VIC)
- Must be an Australian citizen or permanent resident aged 18+
- Must not have previously owned property in Australia
- Must live in the property as your principal place of residence for at least 12 months

**Grant amounts (as at 2024):**
- NSW: $10,000 for new homes up to $600,000 (or $750,000 for new builds)
- VIC: $10,000 for new homes up to $750,000 in metropolitan areas
- QLD: $30,000 for new homes up to $750,000
- SA: $15,000 for new homes up to $650,000
- Other states/territories: Check the relevant state revenue office

**Stamp duty concessions:**
- Most states offer stamp duty concessions or exemptions for first-home buyers
- VIC: Exempt on properties up to $600,000; concession up to $750,000
- NSW: Exempt on properties up to $800,000; concession up to $1,000,000
- Apply through your conveyancer or solicitor at settlement

## Regulatory Information
- All AusFin loan products are issued under the **National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009**
- AusFin holds an Australian Credit Licence (ACL) regulated by **ASIC**
- Responsible lending obligations require AusFin to assess your capacity to repay without substantial hardship
- Comparison rates are calculated on a $150,000 loan over 25 years as required by the **National Credit Code**
- For disputes, contact the **Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA)** on 1800 931 678
