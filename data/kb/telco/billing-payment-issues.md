# Billing and Payment Issues

## Bill Payment Methods
**Available payment options:**
- Online via MyAnyTel app/website
- Direct debit (bank account or credit card)
- BPAY (Biller Code: 12345)
- Phone payment: 131 234
- In-store at AnyTel retail locations

## Late Payment Fees
- $15 fee applies for payments received after due date
- Services may be suspended 14 days after due date
- Reconnection fee: $25

## Disputed Charges
**Common billing disputes:**
- Premium SMS charges: Check for app subscriptions
- Data overage: Review usage in MyAnyTel app
- International charges: Verify roaming was enabled
- Third-party charges: Contact the service provider directly

**Resolution process:**
1. Review detailed bill in MyAnyTel
2. If dispute valid, credit applied within 2-3 business days
3. For complex disputes, escalate to billing specialist

## Payment Plan Options
- Hardship payment plans available
- Minimum $20/week payment required
- No additional fees for approved payment plans
- Contact 131 234 to arrange
