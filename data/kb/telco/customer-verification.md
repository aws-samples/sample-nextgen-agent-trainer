# Customer Verification

## Why Verification Matters

AnyTel is required under the **Telecommunications Consumer Protections (TCP) Code** and the **Privacy Act 1988** to confirm the identity of the account holder before discussing account details or making any changes. Verification protects customers from unauthorised access and protects AnyTel from fraudulent instructions.

Every inbound call must begin with verification — no account information should be shared and no changes made until the customer has been successfully verified.

## Standard Opening Verification

**Ask the customer for two of the following:**

1. Full name on the account
2. Date of birth
3. Service number (mobile number) or account number
4. 4-digit account security PIN
5. Amount of most recent bill (within $5 tolerance)

**Typical opening script:**
> "Before I can access your account, I'll need to confirm a couple of details. Could I please get your full name and your account security PIN?"

Verification must be completed before any account information is displayed or discussed.

## Verification Tiers

### Tier 1 — Basic queries (no account changes)
**Required:** Full name + one of: service number, account number, or DOB

**Permitted actions after Tier 1:**
- Confirm whether an account exists
- Provide general plan and pricing information
- Advise on coverage or network status for a given area
- Direct the customer to self-service options

### Tier 2 — Account queries (view account details)
**Required:** Full name + DOB + service/account number + security PIN

**Permitted actions after Tier 2:**
- View billing details and usage history
- Discuss active plan inclusions and charges
- Explain disputed charges
- Provide estimated bill amounts

### Tier 3 — Account changes
**Required:** All Tier 2 factors + verbal confirmation of intent

**Permitted actions after Tier 3:**
- Change plan or add/remove add-ons
- Set up or cancel direct debit
- Update email address or postal address
- Add or remove an authorised user

### Tier 4 — High-risk changes
**Required:** All Tier 2 factors + one-time password (OTP) sent to the registered mobile number

**Actions requiring Tier 4 (OTP):**
- SIM swap or SIM replacement
- Port-out (transferring number to another carrier)
- Change of registered mobile number or email address
- Request for account closure
- Unlocking a device
- Requesting a call detail record

**OTP process:**
1. Inform the customer an OTP will be sent to their registered mobile
2. Allow 2 minutes for delivery; resend once if not received
3. OTP is valid for 10 minutes
4. If the customer cannot receive the OTP (e.g., SIM is the issue), direct to an AnyTel store with photo ID

## Failed Verification

**If the customer cannot complete Tier 2 verification:**
- Do not share any account details
- Do not confirm whether the service number is active or what plan is on the account
- Advise the customer of alternatives:
  1. Visit an AnyTel store with valid photo ID (driver's licence or passport)
  2. Use the MyAnyTel app password reset (if they have access to the registered email)
  3. Call back when they have their security PIN or bill available

**Script for failed verification:**
> "I'm sorry, I'm unable to verify your identity with the details provided. For security reasons I can't access your account until verification is complete. You're welcome to visit any AnyTel store with your photo ID and we can assist you there."

**Do not:**
- Offer hints or prompts about the missing security details
- Confirm that the account exists at the provided service number
- Make exceptions for customers who claim to be in urgent situations — escalate to a Team Leader if needed

## Third-Party Callers

**Authorised users:**
- An authorised user is someone the account holder has previously nominated to manage the account
- Check the account for an authorised user record before proceeding
- Authorised users complete the same Tier 2 verification using their own name and the account holder's DOB and account number
- Authorised users can make plan changes but **cannot** close the account or request a port-out without account holder consent

**Adding an authorised user:**
- Account holder must call 131 234 and provide the authorised user's full name and DOB
- Changes take effect immediately and are logged on the account

**Callers without authorisation:**
- If someone calls on behalf of the account holder but is not listed as an authorised user, you can only provide general (non-account-specific) information
- Offer to call the account holder directly to confirm authorisation during the call

## Vulnerable Customers

**Signs a customer may need additional support:**
- Distress, confusion, or signs of cognitive difficulty
- Caller appears to be under pressure from someone else in the background
- Unusual urgency to complete a high-risk change

**What to do:**
- Slow down and speak clearly; allow extra time
- Do not proceed with high-risk changes if you have concerns about whether the customer is acting freely and voluntarily
- Escalate to a Team Leader if you are unsure
- Refer to the AnyTel Financial Hardship or Accessibility support line if relevant: 131 234

## Regulatory Context

- **TCP Code (ACMA):** Requires AnyTel to take reasonable steps to verify identity before making account changes
- **Privacy Act 1988 (Cth):** AnyTel must not disclose personal information to a person who has not been verified as the account holder or an authorised representative
- **Telecommunications Industry Ombudsman (TIO):** Customers who believe AnyTel has incorrectly shared their account information without proper verification can lodge a complaint with the TIO on 1800 062 058 or at www.tio.com.au
