# Frequent Flyer Program

## Membership Tiers

**Blue (Entry Level):**
- No minimum status credits required
- Points earning rate: 5 points per $1 spent on eligible SkyAus flights

**Silver:**
- Requires 25,000 status credits earned within a membership year
- Points earning rate: 7 points per $1 spent on eligible SkyAus flights
- Perks: priority check-in and boarding, 1 lounge pass per quarter, 1 free date change per booking

**Gold:**
- Requires 50,000 status credits earned within a membership year
- Points earning rate: 10 points per $1 spent on eligible SkyAus flights
- Perks: 2 free checked bags, unlimited domestic lounge access, priority rebooking, complimentary seat upgrades

**Platinum:**
- Requires 75,000 status credits earned within a membership year
- Points earning rate: 15 points per $1 spent on eligible SkyAus flights
- Perks: international lounge access, guaranteed economy seat on sold-out domestic flights, dedicated Platinum service line

## Points Earning

**Eligible earn categories:**
- SkyAus base fares and fare upgrades
- Partner airline codeshare flights (earn rates vary)
- Partner hotel, car hire, and retail earn (rates vary by partner)

**Points do not accrue on:**
- Taxes, fees, and carrier charges
- Reward seat bookings
- Infant fares

## Points Redemption

**Example reward seat redemption costs:**
- Perth to Sydney return: 6,000 points
- Perth to Singapore return: 9,000 points

**Redemption rules:**
- Points redemptions are subject to reward seat availability
- Points and cash combination bookings are available on selected routes
- Points cannot be redeemed for taxes, fees, or carrier charges — these must be paid in cash

## Missing Points Disputes

**Process for disputed or missing points:**
1. Customer must submit a missing points claim via the SkyAus frequent flyer portal or by calling 1300 SKY AUS
2. Boarding pass, booking reference, and flight details must be provided
3. Claims must be lodged within 12 months of the flight date
4. SkyAus will investigate and respond within 10 business days
5. If claim is validated, points are credited to the account retroactively

**Agent guidance:**
- Agents cannot manually credit points; all missing points claims must go through the formal disputes process
- Advise customers to retain boarding passes and booking confirmations as evidence
