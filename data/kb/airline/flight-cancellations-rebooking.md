# Flight Cancellations and Rebooking

## SkyAus-Initiated Cancellations

**Customer entitlements:**
- Full refund to original payment method, processed within 7 business days
- No-fee rebooking to the next available SkyAus flight on the same route
- Customers may request a travel credit instead of a cash refund

**Rebooking process:**
1. Agent confirms the cancelled flight and customer booking reference
2. Identify next available SkyAus service on the same route
3. Offer rebooking at no additional fare difference for equivalent class
4. If no suitable alternative within 24 hours, offer full refund
5. Document the cancellation reason code for internal reporting

## Customer-Initiated Cancellations

**Flexible Fare:**
- Full refund permitted up to 2 hours before departure
- Refund to original payment method within 7 business days

**Semi-Flex Fare:**
- Cancellation permitted; refund issued minus $75 per sector per passenger change/cancellation fee
- Fare residual refunded to original payment method within 7 business days

**Non-Refundable Economy Fare:**
- No cash refund on voluntary cancellation
- Fare is forfeited; taxes and government charges may be refunded (assessed case by case)
- Medical and compassionate exceptions — refer to Refunds and Compensation policy

## Priority Rebooking by Membership Tier

**Gold members:**
- Priority rebooking on the next available SkyAus service ahead of non-status passengers
- Agents should flag Gold status and action rebooking before general queue processing

**Platinum members:**
- Guaranteed rebooking on next available SkyAus domestic service
- Dedicated Platinum service line available at 1300 SKY AUS
- Platinum members are to be escalated immediately in the event of a cancellation during irregular operations

**Silver members:**
- Priority check-in and boarding on rebooked flight
- 1 free date change per booking already included in Silver benefits; applies here for voluntary changes

**Blue members:**
- Standard rebooking queue; no priority entitlements

## Irregular Operations (IROPS)

**Agent guidance during mass disruption events:**
- Do not make compensation promises beyond the published entitlements
- Meal vouchers and hotel accommodation may be offered for overnight disruptions caused by SkyAus operational issues — supervisor approval required
- Weather, air traffic control, and security events are outside SkyAus control; accommodation and meal entitlements do not automatically apply
